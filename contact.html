Frequently Asked Questions

What are the batch timings?

We offer flexible batch timings including weekday (morning/evening) and weekend batches to accommodate working professionals and students.

Do you provide placement assistance?

Yes, we provide 100% placement assistance including resume preparation, interview training, and direct connections with hiring companies.

What is the duration of courses?

Course duration varies from 2-6 months depending on the program. We also offer fast-track options for quick learning.

Are there any prerequisites?

Most courses are designed for beginners with no prerequisites. Specific requirements, if any, are mentioned in the course details.

Do you offer online training?

Yes, we offer both classroom and online training options with live sessions, recorded lectures, and hands-on labs.

What certifications do you provide?

We provide course completion certificates and also help you prepare for industry-recognized certifications like AWS, RHCE, MCSA, etc.
